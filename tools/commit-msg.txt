Allow multiple clock punches per day (split shifts)

Staff could not clock back in after clocking out because clock_records
had a one-row-per-employee-per-day unique index. Migration 009 replaces
it with a partial unique index that allows any number of completed
punches but still only one open punch at a time.

Staff app Clock screen now shows a per-punch log with a daily total and
offers Clock In Again after clocking out. Admin Employees screen lists
every punch per day with individual edit buttons, an add-punch button,
and per-day totals; on-duty detection now keys off the open punch.
