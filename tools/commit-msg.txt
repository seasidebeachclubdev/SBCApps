Overhaul shift swap, schedule, and payroll logic

Approving a pickup used to move the shift row to the claimer, leaving
the owner with no row on that date, so the weekly-pattern materializer
immediately re-created their shift: both employees ended up scheduled.
Migration 010 adds an atomic approve_pickup() that turns the owner's
row into a cancelled placeholder (blocks the materializer, records who
covers the day) and gives the claimer their own scheduled row, with a
conflict re-check at approval time.

The shifts protect-trigger is now a real state machine. Staff can only
drop their own shift, take back an unclaimed drop, claim someone
else's drop, or withdraw their own claim; times, dates, area, owner,
and approval are manager-only. The approved flag resets automatically
on staff transitions, claim races are rejected instead of silently
overwriting, and the collision check now also counts the claimer's
other pending claims.

Staff app: Swap screen shows pending drops (with Take Back), pending
claims (with Withdraw), and drops a coworker claimed, and surfaces
database errors instead of always reporting success. Schedule shows
this week and next, every shift on a day, and honest status badges.

Admin: pattern edits no longer delete shifts the employee picked up
from someone else, the day editor lists every shift on a date, and
editing a shift no longer silently resets its status. Pay period is
now 14 days everywhere (staff Pay was 30, admin Payroll 15).

tools/test-swap.mjs covers the full lifecycle (13 checks) including
the materializer-resurrection regression.
